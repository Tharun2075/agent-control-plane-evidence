## What the company is

It was originally incorporated as WIDELOGIC LIMITED (12 Nov 1990 to 03 Dec 1990), then traded as ADVANCED RISC MACHINES LIMITED (03 Dec 1990 to 21 May 1998), and has been ARM LIMITED since 21 May 1998. General background, not taken from the register: the Arm name is associated with the Cambridge-based designer and licensor of RISC processor architectures and IP.
