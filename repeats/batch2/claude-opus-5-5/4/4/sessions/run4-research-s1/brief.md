## What the company is
ARM LIMITED is a UK private limited company registered in England and Wales. It was incorporated as WIDELOGIC LIMITED, traded as ADVANCED RISC MACHINES LIMITED from 1990 to 1998, and has been ARM LIMITED since 21 May 1998. It is widely known as the Cambridge-based designer and licensor of Arm processor architectures and intellectual property. This description comes from general knowledge, not from the register entry.
