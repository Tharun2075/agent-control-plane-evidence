## What the company is
ARM LIMITED is a UK private limited company registered in England and Wales. It was originally incorporated as WIDELOGIC LIMITED, renamed ADVANCED RISC MACHINES LIMITED on 3 Dec 1990, and has traded as ARM LIMITED since 21 May 1998. Background (general knowledge, not from the register): Arm is widely known as a designer and licensor of processor architectures and semiconductor IP, headquartered in Cambridge.
