## What the company is
ARM LIMITED is a UK private limited company registered in England and Wales. It was originally incorporated as WIDELOGIC LIMITED and was renamed ADVANCED RISC MACHINES LIMITED shortly afterwards. It has traded as ARM LIMITED since May 1998. It is the UK company behind the Arm processor architecture and licensing business, which is headquartered in Cambridge.
