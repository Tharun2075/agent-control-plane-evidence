## What the company is
ARM LIMITED is a UK private limited company. It was incorporated as WIDELOGIC LIMITED, renamed ADVANCED RISC MACHINES LIMITED on 3 December 1990, and took its current name on 21 May 1998. The facts in this section come from the register.

Background (general knowledge, not from the register): this is understood to be the Cambridge-based UK operating company of the Arm group, which designs and licenses RISC processor architectures and related semiconductor IP.
