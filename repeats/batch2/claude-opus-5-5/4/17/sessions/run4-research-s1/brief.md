## What the company is
ARM LIMITED is a UK private limited company. It was originally incorporated as WIDELOGIC LIMITED. It was renamed ADVANCED RISC MACHINES LIMITED on 3 Dec 1990 and took its current name, ARM LIMITED, on 21 May 1998. This history matches the well-known Cambridge-based Arm semiconductor and processor-architecture business. That link is general background and is not stated on the register.
