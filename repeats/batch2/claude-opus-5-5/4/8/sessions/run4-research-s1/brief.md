## What the company is
ARM LIMITED is a UK private limited company registered in England and Wales. It was incorporated as WIDELOGIC LIMITED and renamed ADVANCED RISC MACHINES LIMITED on 3 Dec 1990. It took its current name on 21 May 1998. Arm is widely known as a designer and licensor of processor (RISC) architectures and IP. Note that this description comes from general knowledge, not from the register.
