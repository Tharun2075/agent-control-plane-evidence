## What the company is
ARM LIMITED is a UK private limited company based in Cambridge. It was originally incorporated as WIDELOGIC LIMITED and renamed ADVANCED RISC MACHINES LIMITED shortly after. It took its current name in 1998. It is the UK company behind the Arm processor architecture business.
