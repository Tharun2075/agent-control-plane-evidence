## Overview
ARM LIMITED is a UK private limited company registered in England and Wales. It was incorporated as WIDELOGIC LIMITED and renamed ADVANCED RISC MACHINES LIMITED in December 1990. It took its current name in May 1998. The company is widely known as the UK operating company of the Arm group, which designs and licenses processor architectures and related semiconductor IP.
